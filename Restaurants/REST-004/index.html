01 · OUR STORY

Where Every Plate
Becomes a Poem

✦

For twenty-seven years, Lumière has pursued a single obsession — the moment a guest closes their eyes at the first bite. Chef Élodie Marchand composes twelve-course odysseys that move like seasons: delicate, fiery, tender, and quietly unforgettable.

Beneath vaulted stone cellars rests a 320-label wine library; above, thirty-two candlelit seats orbit an open flame kitchen where every element is finished before your eyes.

— Élodie Marchand, Chef & Founder

04 · THE MAESTRO

Élodie Marchand

"Cooking is my language — every plate, a sentence in an ongoing love letter to Paris."

Trained under three legends of Lyonnaise cuisine before opening Lumière at twenty-nine, Chef Marchand has spent nearly three decades distilling French tradition into something weightless and new. Her kitchen runs on wood fire, live fermentation, and an unreasonable devotion to detail.

Michelin Guide · Chef of the Year, 2023
Grand Prix de l'Art de la Cuisine
Author of "Feu Doux" — Éditions du Goût
