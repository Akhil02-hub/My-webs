Our Story

A Legacy Painted in Spice & Colour

Rangoli was born from the childhood memories of Chef Arjun Mehra — of his grandmother laying coloured powders at the doorstep every Diwali in Jaipur, and of the kitchens of Lucknow where the slow art of dum pukht was perfected over centuries.

Today, our kitchen unites royal recipes from Awadh, Hyderabad, Kashmir and the Malabar coast with the finest British produce. Every masala is ground by hand each morning. Every plate is a canvas.

More Than a Meal — an Evening to Remember

From the moment the brass doors open to the final sip of chai, every detail is choreographed. Hand-painted frescoes, live sitar on Fridays, and a sommelier team celebrated for their Indian-wine pairings.
