Full‑stack restaurant website for **Foodies** – a bakery, fast food, cakes, Chinese, and biryani spot located in **Kodad, Telangana**.  
Customers can explore the menu, book a table, leave reviews, and browse the gallery. Owners can manage everything through a secure admin dashboard.

🔗 **Live Demo**: https://foodieskodad.onrender.com

---

## ✨ Features

- 🍽️ **Dynamic Menu** – Categories (Biryani, Chinese, Fast Food, Cakes, Bakery, Beverages) with search & filters.
- ⭐ **Signature Favourites** – Showcase chef‑special or popular dishes on the homepage.
- 📅 **Table Booking** – Customers book tables; bookings are saved with a unique ID and status (Pending / Confirmed / Cancelled).
- ⭐ **Customer Reviews** – Submit star ratings and comments; average rating displayed.
- 🖼️ **Gallery** – Upload and manage food, cake, bakery, and restaurant photos with lightbox.
- 🛠️ **Admin Dashboard** – Full CRUD for menu items, signature favourites, gallery, bookings, and reviews.
- 📱 **Responsive** – Works perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- 🔐 **Admin Key Protection** – Simple password protection for the admin panel.
