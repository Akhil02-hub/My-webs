Our Story • Kalwakurthy

A Small Town's Big Biryani Legend.

Located on Main Road, opposite the Bus Stand, Ginger House started as a 10-table eatery in 2012. Today it's the go-to halt for families, wedding parties and Hyderabad–Srisailam highway travellers craving Kalwakurthy's most fragrant dum biryani.

Every handi is layered with aged basmati, saffron milk, fried onions and our secret ginger-garam masala — sealed with dough and slow-cooked. No colours, no shortcuts.
