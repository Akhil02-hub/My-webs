The Heritage Method

The Art of Slow "Dum Pukht"

Where Persian culinary aesthetics meet rich Indian royal spices. We never rush. We let time create perfection.

01

Pehli Dawat (Marination)

Meat massaged with freshly pound javitri, shahi jeera, cold-pressed mustard oil, sour curd and crushed garlic ginger overnight.

02

Birinj (Aged Basmati)

2-year extra-long grain rice parboiled in cardamom & whole spice broth to exactly 70% tenderness before layering.

03

Pardah (Dough Seal)

Heavy terracotta handi is sealed with whole wheat dough ring to lock every drop of aromatic spice vapor inside.

04

Koyla Dum (Gentle Fire)

Cooked upon gentle glowing wood charcoal embers, infusing subtle smoke until unsealed directly at your dining table.
