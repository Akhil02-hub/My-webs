Our Heritage

A Legacy of
Royal Flavors

Born from the kitchens of the Nizams of Hyderabad, Premum carries forward a 300-year-old tradition of dum biryani. Our master chefs, trained in the royal kitchens, bring you an experience that transcends ordinary dining.

Every pot is sealed with hand-rolled atta dough, slow-cooked on charcoal fire for hours, and opened tableside — releasing an aroma that has enchanted royalty for generations.
