Where is Foodies located in Kodad?

Foodies is located at Kodad Main Road, beside Ranga Theater, Kodad, Telangana 508206.

Does Foodies Kodad deliver?

Yes! You can order online via Tizola, or call us directly for takeaway and delivery options.

What are Foodies Kodad timings?

We are open Monday to Sunday, from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
