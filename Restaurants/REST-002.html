Our Story

Founded in 2018, Savor & Vine was born from a passion for bringing people together through exceptional food. Our head chef, Maria Rossi, brings 15 years of culinary experience from top restaurants across Europe, blending traditional techniques with modern innovation.

We source 90% of our ingredients from local farms and producers, ensuring every dish is fresh, sustainable, and bursting with flavor. Whether you're joining us for a casual weeknight dinner or a special celebration, we promise an unforgettable dining experience.
