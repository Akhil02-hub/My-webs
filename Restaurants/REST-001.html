About Abhishta Restaurant

A Welcoming Oasis for Hungry Travelers

Whether you are embarking on a sacred pilgrimage to the Mallikarjuna Jyotirlinga Temple at Srisailam or cruising back towards Hyderabad, ABHISHTA Multicuisine Restaurant in Raghaipally (Amangal) is designed to be your favorite haven.

After enduring the long 60+ km dense jungle stretch, our diners enjoy delicious freshly cooked hot meals. We take pride in serving high-grade vegetarian and non-vegetarian delicacies, cooked separately with absolute hygiene, rapid service, and authentic spices.
