Our Story

Cuisine born of patience and the seasons

Chef Élise Moreau opened Maison Lumière with a single conviction: that luxury is found in restraint. Each dish begins at dawn in the markets of Rungis, where we choose produce at the peak of its life, then honour it with technique refined over two decades in the kitchens of Lyon and Kyoto.

Our nine-course tasting menu changes with the moon and the harvest — never the same twice, always unmistakably ours.
