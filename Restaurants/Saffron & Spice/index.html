Our Heritage

A Family Legacy of Flavor

Founded in 1987 by Master Chef Rajesh Sharma, Saffron & Spice began as a humble family kitchen in Old Delhi. Today, we bring you three generations of culinary wisdom, blending traditional recipes with contemporary elegance.

Every spice is hand-selected, every tandoor is stoked with care, and every plate tells a story of India's rich, diverse gastronomic tapestry.
