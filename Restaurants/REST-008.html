Our story

A modern palace for India’s finest flavors.

Inspired by the grand dining halls of Jaipur, Hyderabad, Lucknow, and Mumbai, Saagar Mahal blends time-honored techniques with contemporary presentation.

Our chefs grind spices daily, slow-cook sauces overnight, and finish select dishes over charcoal, copper handi, or tandoor flame. Every plate is designed to feel generous, precise, and unforgettable.
