# 🌐 My Webs

A collection of **modern, responsive, and professionally designed websites** built by **Akhil** across different industries and use cases.

This repository serves as a central portfolio of my web development projects, from simple landing pages to full-stack web applications.

## 🚀 Website Categories

### 1. 💼 Portfolio

Personal portfolios, developer portfolios, creative portfolios, and professional profile websites.

### 2. 🍽️ Restaurants

Restaurant, café, food, dhaba, and dining websites with modern menus, galleries, bookings, and contact features.

### 3. 🏨 Hotels

Hotel, resort, hospitality, and accommodation websites designed to showcase rooms, services, facilities, and booking information.

### 4. 🏢 Business

Business websites for companies, agencies, startups, local businesses, and professional services.

### 5. 🛒 E-Commerce

Online stores and shopping websites with product catalogs, carts, authentication, and other e-commerce functionality.

### 6. 🎓 Education

Educational websites for schools, colleges, courses, coaching centers, academies, and learning platforms.

### 7. 🚀 Landing Pages

Modern landing pages created for products, services, businesses, campaigns, startups, and promotions.

### 8. 🎨 Other

Other creative and custom websites that do not fit into the categories above.
