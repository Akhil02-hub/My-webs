WELCOME TO GATE KODAD

Engineering minds,
building careers.

At Gandhi Academy of Technical Education, we believe that education extends far beyond the classroom. For over three decades, we have fostered an inclusive, challenging, and supportive environment where students are encouraged to ask questions, think critically, and act with compassion.

Our dedicated faculty combines innovative teaching methods with timeless values, ensuring that our graduates are not only academically prepared but also equipped to make a positive impact in a rapidly changing world.
