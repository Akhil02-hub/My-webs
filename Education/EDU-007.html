About Sana Engineering College

A Legacy of Technical Excellence

Sana Engineering College (SEC), established in 2003 in Kodad, Suryapet District, is a distinguished engineering institution committed to providing quality technical and management education.

Spread over an expansive green campus along NH-65, our institution provides well-equipped laboratories, digital libraries, modern computer centers, and an active training & placement cell that connects students with top industries.
