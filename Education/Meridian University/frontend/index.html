Our Heritage

A Legacy of Rigorous Curiosity

Founded in 1887 on three hundred acres of New England woodland, Meridian University began with a single hall, eleven students, and an audacious belief: that education should transform not just what you know, but who you become.

Today, that founding conviction animates six schools, 140+ programs of study, and a community of scholars drawn from 96 countries — all bound by the pursuit of veritas et virtus, truth and character.

Questions, Answered.

Our admissions team responds within one business day. Prefer to talk? Call (800) 555-1887 Monday through Friday.

Early Action: November 15 (decisions mid-December). Regular Decision: January 15 (decisions late March). Transfer applications close March 1. All deadlines are 11:59 PM in your local timezone.

Admission is need-blind for all applicants, and we meet 100% of demonstrated need without loans. Families earning under $85,000 typically pay nothing. The average grant last year was $52,400.

Meridian is permanently test-optional. SAT and ACT scores are considered only if you choose to submit them — roughly 58% of enrolled students in the Class of 2029 did so.
