Full Biography

Alex Rivera is a Principal AI Engineer at xAI where he leads the development of advanced reasoning systems for Grok. Previously he spent three years at Anthropic building the Claude family of models and two years at OpenAI contributing to the GPT-4 training cluster and alignment research.

His research interests include scalable oversight, synthetic data generation, and emergent capabilities in frontier models. He holds a PhD from Stanford where he studied under Prof. Christopher Manning and was awarded the Best Thesis in AI award in 2018.

When not training models, Alex can be found trail running in Marin, playing jazz piano, or experimenting with generative music systems.
