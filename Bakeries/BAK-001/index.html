Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Innovation

Started in 1985 as a small family-run shop in Mumbai, The Cardamom Crumb has grown into a beloved destination for those who crave the nostalgic flavors of India, reimagined with modern baking techniques.

Every morning, our master bakers combine locally-sourced ingredients, aromatic spices, and time-honored recipes to create breads, cakes, and sweets that tell a story.
