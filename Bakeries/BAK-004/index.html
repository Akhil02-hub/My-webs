Our Story

Three generations of baking tradition

Baking with Love Since 1965

Sweet Memories Bakery began as a small family-owned shop in the heart of Mumbai. Our founder, Shri Ramesh Patel, started with a simple dream — to bring authentic Indian flavors to every celebration.

Today, we continue that legacy by blending time-honored recipes with modern techniques, using only the finest ingredients sourced from trusted Indian farmers.
