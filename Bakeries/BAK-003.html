Our story

Rooted in memory.
Baked for today.

It began with our nani's rose-scented kitchen, a battered recipe diary, and a belief that the best food always carries a story.

Today, Gulab & Grain brings that warmth to a modern neighbourhood bakery. We pair time-honoured Indian flavours with careful technique—kneading, folding and frosting everything by hand.
