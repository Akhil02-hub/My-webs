Our Story

Flour, water, salt —
and twelve years of stubborn patience.

What started as a single wood-fired oven and a folding table at the farmers' market is now the neighborhood's morning ritual. We still mill heritage grains in-house, still ferment every loaf for two full days, and still believe a croissant should shatter when you bite it.

No shortcuts. No preservatives. Just craft you can taste.

Heritage grains, ground fresh weekly

The Craft

Four steps. Two days.
One perfect loaf.

01

Mix & Autolyse

Flour and water rest together before anything else touches the bowl — enzymes wake up, gluten begins forming on its own.

02

Slow Ferment

Forty-eight hours in the cold. Wild yeast works slowly, building the deep, tangy flavor no commercial yeast can fake.

03

Hand Shape

Every loaf is shaped by hand — tensioned, tucked and scored with a signature ear that opens like a smile in the oven.

04

Hearth Bake

Straight onto stone at 480°F with a burst of steam. The crust crackles as it cools — bakers call it singing.
